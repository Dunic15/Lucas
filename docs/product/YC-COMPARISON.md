## 1. The verdict in three sentences

The two source docs disagree on the #1 niche, and the newer, verified one wins:
**YC-NICHE-MINING ranked Recruiting #1 on WTP + validation; the adversarially-verified
CATALOG demotes it to #2 and promotes Sales B2B consultivo to #1 as whitespace.**
The reason is a change of discriminant: not "which lane has the highest willingness to
pay" (every lane does), but **"is the exact behavior, an avatar that sits live in the
customer's own meeting AND executes the vertical's actions, still unoccupied?"** On

## 2. Reconciling the tension: which ranking wins, and why

| | YC-NICHE-MINING (older memo) | YC-COMPANIES-CATALOG (verified census) |
|---|---|---|
| **Method** | Score lanes on WTP × meeting/action density × approachability × validation | Company-level census + adversarial verification (each claim gets 3 votes; unproven claims killed) |
| **#1** | Recruiting / Staffing (23/25) | **Sales B2B consultivo; whitespace** |
| **#2** | Consultative B2B Sales (21/25) | **Recruiting / Staffing via the agency/ATS buyer** |
| **Discriminant** | "highest willingness-to-pay + most-validated wallet" | **"is the avatar-that-executes behavior still free in this lane?"** |

**Why the CATALOG ranking wins on the evidence.** The niche-mining memo optimizes for a
wallet that is proven to open. But a proven wallet with a dozen funded YC startups already
reaching for it is a *red ocean*, not an opening. The catalog re-frames the question around
the one thing Laura can actually own, the exact *behavior*, and finds:

- **Sales:** the only company ever claimed to be an avatar live on a sales call, **Caretta**
  ("Sales Intelligence… joins reps in live calls"), **failed adversarial verification 0-3**.
  No confirmed competitor does "avatar-live-on-the-call + executes." **True whitespace.**
- **Recruiting:** the identical motion is occupied. **Alex** (W24, ex-Apriora, $17M) runs
  *live video interviews* + one-click ATS write-back; **Lightscreen AI** (F24) does
  full-length voice+video interviews; **Peoplebox Nova** (S22) does pre-screen/first-round
  interviews. **Red ocean for the identical product.**

So the memo's #1 (Recruiting) is where the *wallet* is most validated, and the catalog's #1
(Sales) is where the *behavior* is most unoccupied. **The behavior test is the one that maps
to defensibility**, because a highly-validated wallet you enter as competitor #13 is worth
less than a genuinely empty lane; the census wins. (Two honesty caveats, both from the
catalog: the recruiting interviewers run on *their own* video surface, not the client's
Zoom/Meet, so "join the calendar meeting" is slightly *less* occupied than raw counts imply;
and Sales' whitespace comes with *less validation of this exact motion*: see §4.)

## 4. Where Laura is differentiated vs. where it's commoditized

**Be blunt: two of the three things people assume are Laura's edge are already commodities.**

| Layer | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **The photoreal face** | ❌ NOT a moat | Keyframe Labs sells real-time photoreal avatar as SDK infra at **$0.06/min**. Laura's `/talk` path is *cheaper still* (zero face-vendor cost, ~$0.40 per 30-min call), but "having a good face" is a purchasable input, not defensibility. |
| **Generic post-meeting automation** | ❌ NOT a moat | Circleback (W24) already auto-creates tasks, updates the CRM, and posts recaps. Horizontal follow-up execution is a shipped, funded commodity. |
| **Meeting plumbing** (join / transcribe / render) | ❌ NOT a moat | Bought from Recall.ai and swappable by design (per CONTEXT.md / WEDGE.md). |
| **Vertical ownership + executing the vertical's exact actions LIVE, in the client's own meeting** | ✅ The defensible position | No confirmed competitor occupies "avatar-in-the-meeting-that-executes" (§3). Laura's edge is the stack **grounded live answers → deterministic live missing-step flag → vertical action execution**, in a lane where that combined behavior is unoccupied. |

**The one-line honest version:** Laura does not win because it has a face or because it emails
a recap. It wins **only** if it owns a vertical, its process templates, its knowledge pack,
its exact action set, and executes that vertical's actions *live, inside the customer's own
meeting*, in a lane where no one else does. If Laura competes on the face or on generic
automation, it loses to a $0.06/min SDK and to Circleback respectively.

## 5. Recommendation (the owner's call: Duccio / Christian / Jacopo)

**Data-backed lean: enter on the Sales-whitespace behavior, hedged by the Recruiting-validated
wallet; and pick the entry buyer deliberately.** Both are defensible *if and only if* Laura
executes the vertical actions live; the difference is which risk you'd rather carry.

**The honest tradeoff:**

- **Sales B2B consultivo (catalog #1): TRUE whitespace, THINNER validation of this exact
  motion.** Upside: no confirmed avatar-in-the-call competitor (Caretta refuted); high WTP
  (AEs/SEs at $150–300k OTE); low regulation. Risk: the "expert answer" is the *customer's
  own* playbook (a per-customer knowledge pack, not one shared vertical pack), the horizontal
  lane is crowded above the avatar layer (Gong, native Zoom/Teams AI), and *this specific
  motion* (avatar in the deal call executing CRM hygiene) has less proof that buyers will
  pay for it; because no one has sold it yet. Whitespace cuts both ways.

- **Recruiting / Staffing (memo #1, catalog #2): HUGE validated wallet, CROWDED.** Upside:
  the single most-validated vertical-AI wallet at YC (Alex $17M, Juicebox ~$10M ARR/$30M
  raise, Contrario ~$6M). Risk: red ocean for the identical product, plus the EU AI Act
  high-risk shadow. **Do NOT enter candidate screening; that's Alex/Lightscreen/Peoplebox.**
  Enter through **(a) the staffing-firm / ATS buyer** (Spott proves that wallet is real and
  paid) and **(b) the recruiter's INTERNAL meetings**, the client/hiring-manager **intake
  call** and the **interview debrief/calibration**, where the expensive humans meet, the
  talk is candid, and the follow-ups pile up. That slice dodges the crowd *and* the regulator.

**Recommended posture:** treat **Sales as the primary whitespace bet** and the **recruiter's
internal intake/debrief (agency/ATS buyer) as the validated hedge**: because the four-to-six
actions below are *common to both*, so shipping them keeps both doors open until a design
partner in one lane forces the choice. The one thing that is NOT a valid option is entering
either lane on the face or on generic post-meeting automation.

**The 4–6 vertical actions to ship (common to Sales AND Recruiting-internal):**
1. **Join** the client's Zoom/Meet/Teams call and answer **grounded** from the customer's
   process/product docs (shipped: Recall + RAG).
2. **Flag a missing qualification LIVE**, no budget owner / no decision date (Sales); a
   skipped must-have competency (Recruiting), Laura's deterministic closing intervention,
   retargeted (shipped).
3. **Send the recap / next-step email** (draft shipped; auto-send is the executor's Level-B
   flip).
4. **Auto-schedule the next step**, technical deep-dive / POC kickoff (Sales); interview
   round / panel (Recruiting), Google Calendar write (executor foundations shipped, flag-off).
5. **Write to the CRM/ATS**: stage, next step, close date (HubSpot/Salesforce) or req,
   stage move, scorecard (Ashby/Greenhouse/Bullhorn). *This is the vertical-specific build
   not yet done; it is the action that makes the wedge real.*
6. **Route** a follow-up to the right internal owner (security/pricing question, or overdue
   interviewer feedback / stalled req).

## 6. So what: for the roadmap (3 bullets the roadmap can consume)

- **The niche decision (`ROADMAP` LATER #14) should be re-cut on the behavior test, not the
  WTP test.** Recruiting is the validated wallet but red-ocean; **Sales is the true
  whitespace** because Caretta was refuted. Recommend Sales-primary / Recruiting-internal-hedge,
  entered via the staffing-firm/ATS buyer and internal intake/debrief; never candidate
  screening. Owner (Duccio/Christian/Jacopo) makes the final call.
- **The CRM/ATS write action is the single highest-leverage build (`ROADMAP` NOW #1 → LATER
  #15).** Grounded answers, live flag, draft email, and Calendar/Gmail write are shipped or
  flag-off; **system-of-record write is the missing cell that turns "notetaker with a face"
  into "avatar that executes the vertical."** It is common to both candidate lanes, so it's
  not blocked on the niche decision; build it next.
- **Kill the "face is the moat" and "automation is the moat" narratives in every artifact.**
  Keyframe ($0.06/min) commoditizes the face and Circleback commoditizes generic follow-up.
  Positioning, deck, and landing copy must lead with **vertical ownership + live in-meeting
  execution**, and treat the photoreal face as a cheap bought input, not the pitch.
