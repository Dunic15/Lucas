## 0. The two corrections this research forced (read first)

The first-pass catalog had two claims this deeper verification **changes**. Both keep the
headline ("Sales avatar-in-call = whitespace") intact but fix the *reason*: and the fixed
version is the more defensible one under an advisor's scrutiny.

1. **Caretta (YC W26) is NOT "refuted 0-3": it is real but *adjacent*.** Its own YC launch
   page confirms it is a **rep-side private copilot that lives in Slack** ("if it detects a
   rep needs help, it brings up answers… after the call, Caretta joins your team in Slack") -
   **no face, not a speaking participant, no CRM/scheduling execution.** Same lane as Clari
   Copilot. So the whitespace holds because **no one does the exact behavior**, not because
   the one claimant was fake. Wording: **"confirmed real, confirmed adjacent."**
2. **CRM/ATS write is now table-stakes: necessary, not sufficient.** A cluster already does
   "join the call + write the CRM" silently: **Coffee** (joins Zoom/Teams/Meet as a
   participant, maps to BANT/MEDDIC), **Attio** (bot writes summaries into deal records),
   **Fireflies** (auto-creates the CRM contact), **Gong** ("AI Data Extractor" writes CRM
   fields), **Circleback** (post-meeting CRM/Linear/Slack). The catalog called CRM-write "the
   missing cell that makes the wedge real"; it is now **the price of entry, not the moat.**
   The moat is the **combination** (speaking vertical expert × grounded answers × live flag ×
   *deep* vertical write), not the write alone.

## 1. The AI wave in YC, by batch: copilot → vertical agent → AI employee

The *type* of AI company YC funds moved in three phases; the inflection is sharp across 2024→2025.

| Phase | Batches | What YC funded | Framing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Copilot / assistant** | W22–S23 → into W24 | Tools that help a human work faster (dev copilots, writing/CS assistants) | "AI as a feature" |
| **Vertical AI agent** | W24 → F24 → Spring'25 | Domain-specific agents that take actions in one function/industry | "AI as an app" |
| **AI employee / services-as-software** | S25 → F25 → W26 → Spring'26 | Sell the *outcome*, replace the outsourced service | "AI as a workforce" |

**The 3-denominator correction (do not conflate).** Reported "AI share" numbers mean three
different things and are quoted interchangeably:
- **AI-labeled / "incorporates AI" (broad):** ~66% (W24) → **~90–92%** (S25/F25). Real but soft.
- **"AI agent" companies:** **~46–50%** by Spring'25 (67/144; PitchBook's "all-in on agents").
- **"Agentic / autonomous":** **~30%** of the AI cos (Spring'25).

For Laura the decision-useful number is the **AI-agent (~50%) / agentic (~30%)** cut; that
narrower lane is where the crowding is. The W26 "~60% AI" vs S25 "~91%" gap is a definition
difference, **not** a decline.

**Inflection batches to name in any deck:**
1. **W24**: the vertical-agent turn begins (also where the meeting-AI + recruiting-interviewer cohort appears: Circleback W24, Alex W24).
2. **Spring 2025**: the agent tipping point (~half the batch is "AI agents"; PitchBook "all-in").
3. **S25**: the "AI-employee / services-as-software" entrenchment (sell the service, not the tool).
4. **W26**: the **agent-*infrastructure* boom (41.5%)**: the app layer is assumed; competition moves to picks-and-shovels.

## 2. Which SERVICE verticals YC poured into: and when

Verticals heat in a rough order set by data-cleanliness + clear ROI, then saturate.

| Vertical | Heated | Status now | Tie to census |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Support / CS-AI** | 2022–23 (earliest) | Mature, commoditized | Sierra/Decagon set the bar |
| **Coding / dev agents** | 2023 → exploded 2024–25 | Saturated, still funded | Biggest single app category (F25: 21 cos) |
| **Sales-AI** | 2024 wave | Crowded *above* the avatar layer | Census ~109 cos; **avatar-live-on-call still whitespace** |
| **Recruiting-AI** | 2024 (Alex W24, Lightscreen F24) → 2025 | Most-validated **and** most-saturated | Census 66; identical live-interview+ATS = red-ocean |
| **Legal-AI** | Accelerating 2025 → W26 cluster | Hot, still opening | Census 54; late-heating (regulatory friction) |
| **Finance / Accounting-AI** | 2024 → saturated 2025 | Red-ocean | Census **114; most crowded lane** |
| **Insurance brokerage** | 2025 (YC RFS target) | Opening but **licensed** | Census 54; licensing friction |
| **Healthcare admin** | 2025 (YC RFS target) | Opening | Census marks directional/under-covered |
| **Compliance / tax / audit** | 2025–26 RFS | Emerging | The "sell the service" sweet spot |

**Macro read for Laura:** the lanes that heated *first* (Support, Sales, Recruiting, Finance)
are the ones the census marks *most saturated*. The lanes YC is *now* pointing at (insurance,
healthcare-admin, compliance, legal) are 2025–26 openings **but carry licensing/regulatory
friction**: which is why they were late, and why the 14/07 "no compliance niche" rule points
away from them. The census-recommended lanes (**Sales-whitespace / Recruiting-internal via the
ATS buyer**) sit in the *early-heated-but-validated* zone: the bet is "own an unoccupied
**behavior** inside an already-validated wallet," not "ride a fresh vertical wave."

## 3. The four waves adjacent to Laura, and whether each is now table-stakes

**(a) Voice / speech-to-speech agents, 2024–25 explosion → table-stakes.** Voice-AI funding
rose **~8× 2023→2024 to ~$2.1B**. Vapi **$50M Series B @ ~$500M** (1B+ calls, 1M+ devs); Retell
**~$50M ARR** on ~$5.1M raised. Enabler: OpenAI **Realtime API (Oct 2024)**, gpt-realtime GA,
Gemini Live. **Verdict:** a purchasable primitive ($0.01–0.10/min); an input Laura buys.

**(b) Real-time photoreal avatars: 2024→2025 productization → commoditized.** Real-time
conversational avatars only *launched* mid-2024 (HeyGen Interactive Avatar Jul 23 2024; Tavus
CVI 2024). By Jan 2026 a **10-vendor race spans $0.009–$0.35/min** (Simli $0.009, bitHuman
~$0.01–0.04, Keyframe "$0.06" [directional], Anam $0.11–0.16, Tavus ~$0.32, D-ID ~$0.35). Tavus
raised a **$40M Series B (Nov 2025)**; Lemon Slice **$10.5M (YC/Matrix, Dec 2025)**: capital
chasing the commodity. **Verdict:** a good face is a purchasable input, sub-cent at the floor.

**(c) Meeting-bot / notetaker / native meeting-AI; the horizontal use case, being eaten.**
Recall.ai (YC W20) is the rail; and **the exact moment "an avatar that talks *in* the meeting"
became an API was Recall's Output Media API, Nov 14 2024.** Recall *cut* price to **$0.50/hr**
in 2026; **Vexa** (Apache-2.0) open-sources it at $0.30/hr. Notetakers commoditized each other
to ~free (Fathom unlimited, Otter, Fireflies, Granola). And the **natives climbed from summary →
agentic action**: Zoom AI Companion **free since Sep 2023** → **3.0 "conversations into action"
(2025)**; Google "Take notes for me" **bundled into Workspace Jan 2025 (110M+ MAU, 8.5× YoY)**;
Microsoft **Teams Facilitator**: a *live in-meeting* chat agent (Ignite 2025). **Verdict:**
join + transcribe + summarize + generic follow-up is plumbing you buy or that ships native.

**(d) Agentic execution (agents that DO); the 2025–26 headline wave.** Spring'25 ~half the
batch is "AI agents"; S25/F25 = AI-employee; W26 = 41.5% agent-*infra*. Enabler: **Model Context
Protocol (MCP, Nov 2024)** standardized agent→CRM/calendar/email tool-calling (even added to
OpenAI's Realtime API). **Verdict:** "an agent that acts" is now a commodity *narrative*; the
still-open slice is "*this* agent that acts correctly in *this* vertical, live in the customer's
own meeting."

## 4. "Why now": and why it is not "why us"

Four enablers converged in a ~6-week window (**Oct–Nov 2024**), and **all four equally arm competitors**:

| Enabler | What changed | Date | Also arms rivals |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Speech-to-speech models** | OpenAI Realtime API → sub-second spoken turns | Oct 2024 | ✅ any seed team |
| **Meeting-in/out as a product** | **Recall Output Media API**, "avatar that talks in a Zoom/Meet/Teams call" as an API call | **Nov 14 2024** | ⚠️ **sharpest, hands every competitor the exact plumbing** |
| **Tool-calling standard (MCP)** | Standardized agent→CRM/calendar/email execution | Nov 2024 | ✅ enables everyone's write layer |
| **Latency + cost floor** | Fast inference (Cerebras/Groq) + cheap render/voice → always-on avatar affordable (~$0.40/30-min vs $7–12 human) | 2024→2025 | ✅ same vendors |

**Blunt conclusion:** avatar-in-the-meeting-that-executes became feasible in late 2024; the same
three releases that make Laura possible make the **plumbing non-defensible**. "Why now" is real,
but it is **not "why us."** The only "why us" is knowledge + accuracy + vertical templates +
vertical execution + when-to-speak judgment.

## 6. Whitespace durability: holding at the intersection, narrowing on every flank

**Verdict: HOLDING at the exact-behavior intersection, NARROWING fast on the flanks.** As of
mid-2026, **no confirmed company does the full stack**: a *speaking* photoreal/voice **expert**
that joins the **client's own** Zoom/Meet/Teams as a third participant, answers **grounded** from
the customer's process docs, **flags a missing step LIVE**, and **writes the vertical's
system-of-record**. That precise intersection is empty. But every *individual* component has now
shipped somewhere, so the wedge is an **intersection of commoditizing parts**, not a single
unclaimed capability.

**Who occupies the flanks (verified, not marketing):**
- **Plumbing / face:** Recall Output Media, **Beyond Presence** (verified dev avatar infra /
  own-surface; its "joins Zoom" is blog hype, no CRM), HeyGen personal-twin, Vexa (OSS).
- **Rep-side live copilot:** **Caretta** (Slack whisper), Clari Copilot (rep battlecards); not speaking participants.
- **Join + write CRM (silent):** Coffee, Attio, Fireflies, Gong, Circleback.
- **Recruiting interviewers (own-surface, candidate-facing):** Alex ($17M), HeyMilo ($6M, 1M+ candidates), Lightscreen, ConverzAI, Ribbon, Peoplebox. **none joins the client's own meeting** → hardens "enter via the recruiter's *internal* intake/debrief, not screening."
- **Native live agents:** Google Meet notetaker; **Teams Facilitator** (live chat agent, nudges absent invitees); **Zoom AI Companion 3.0** (agentic, cross-platform, + Zoom building meeting avatars / Yuan's "digital twin"). **None is a speaking vertical expert grounded in the customer's docs that flags live + writes a vertical SoR**: that gap is Laura's sharpest edge.

**Time horizon:**
- **Horizontal version** (generic "expert avatar in any meeting") → likely **closes in ~12–24 months**, killed by a native platform (Zoom/Microsoft) adding voice-as-distinct-expert + vertical writes to its already-agentic in-meeting assistant. *Highest-probability, fastest closer.*
- **Deep-vertical version** (owns one vertical's process templates + SoR write + the speaking/live-flag motion) → defensible **~2–4 years**, **only if Laura actually verticalizes**: the natives will do horizontal-generic and won't chase staffing intake→Bullhorn or consultative-sales MEDDIC→close-plan.

**What Laura must own (blunt):**
- **NOT a moat:** the face, generic post-meeting automation, **and now CRM/ATS write by itself**.
- **The moat = the intersection, owned deeply in ONE vertical:** (1) vertical process templates + action set; (2) grounded answer accuracy from the customer's docs (the layer the plumbing vendors *explicitly leave open*); (3) the **speaking-expert-participant + deterministic live missing-step flag** (no native or copilot ships this); (4) **depth** of the SoR write (correct MEDDIC/BANT or req/stage/scorecard mapping), coupled to the speaking + flag motion.

**Bottom line:** the census's headline ("Sales is whitespace") **still holds**, but the reason
shifted from *"nobody joins + executes"* to *"nobody joins as a **speaking vertical expert that
flags live AND executes**."* Laura's window is real but is a **narrowing intersection of
commoditizing parts**: defended only by going **deep in one vertical before a native platform
makes the horizontal version a free checkbox.** Pick the lane before Zoom picks it for you.

## 7. So-what for the roadmap (consumable bullets)

- **The niche decision is now *time-boxed*, not open-ended.** The horizontal whitespace closes in
  ~12–24 months. Committing ONE vertical (Sales-whitespace or Recruiting-internal) and going deep
  is the defense: Jacopo's YC batch-scrape should *confirm-and-commit*, not defer indefinitely.
- **Re-price the CRM/ATS write item.** It is no longer "the cell that makes the wedge real" (it's
  table-stakes now); it's **necessary but not sufficient**. Prioritize it, but the differentiator
  to build and pitch is the **combination**: speaking vertical expert + grounded answers + live
  missing-step flag + *deep* vertical write. Depth of the write (vertical field mapping) > the write.
- **State the native-platform threat in the deck and defend against it by verticalizing.** Zoom
  AI Companion 3.0 / Teams Facilitator are 1–2 product decisions from the horizontal version. Kill
  "face is the moat" and "automation is the moat" everywhere; lead with vertical ownership + live
  in-meeting execution.
