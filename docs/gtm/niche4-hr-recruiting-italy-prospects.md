# Niche 4 (Italy) — HR, Recruiting & Talent Agency Prospects

> Refinement of "Niche 4 — HR, recruiting & talent agencies" in
> `target-accounts-clay-brief.md`, narrowed to **Italy-only** and resolved to
> **named, verified companies** (the original brief was Clay search-criteria
> only, no names). Pure prospecting research — no positioning/messaging here.

## Methodology

Every company below was found via live web search (English + Italian queries
across Milan, Rome, Turin, Bologna, Florence, Veneto/Padua, Brescia, Naples,
Sardinia) and cross-checked against its own website + LinkedIn company page
(or third-party data aggregators — RocketReach, ZoomInfo, Clutch, The
Manifest, ensun — when LinkedIn didn't surface a headcount). Employee counts
vary between sources (LinkedIn self-reported ranges vs. registry/revenue data
vs. aggregator estimates); where I could not converge on a confident number
I say so explicitly rather than guess.

**Screened out during research (not included below, for transparency):**
- **Alti Profili** — a *division of Umana S.p.A.*, a large Italian staffing
  agency (~1,000+ direct employees, 150+ branches) — excluded as a
  subsidiary of a giant staffing corp.
- **ROMA Hired** — name suggests Rome, but it's actually incorporated and
  based in Chicago, Illinois — not genuinely Italy-HQ'd.
- **in.HR Agenzia per il Lavoro** — LinkedIn lists 501–1,000 employees
  group-wide — too large for this brief.
- **Zero to One Search**, **Talentuch** — real Milan/Vanzago presence but
  actually HQ'd in Germany and the US/Ukraine respectively — not genuine
  Italy HQs.
- **Eitos**, **Ricercamy**, **Studio Pavan**, **Artemide Recruitment** (HQ
  London) — either too small (2–10, no clear team beyond founder+few) or not
  Italy-HQ'd.
- **ALI S.p.A.** — legitimate Italian firm but 589–1,000+ employees, scale
  comparable to the excluded staffing giants.
