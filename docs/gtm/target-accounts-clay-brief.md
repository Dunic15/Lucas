## Global settings (apply to every niche)

- **Geography priority:** Italy, France, Spain, Germany, UK, Switzerland,
  Netherlands, plus broader EU/US and international remote-first startups or
  consultancies. Rank higher if HQ or a real office sits in the priority list.
- **Exclude:** companies over 1,000 employees (unless a clearly separate
  innovation lab / digital unit); companies with no evident recurring
  meeting-heavy workflow; students, interns, or freelancers without a team;
  companies whose core need is transcription only, not process/execution
  (e.g. happy Otter/Fireflies/Grain users with no ops pain).
- **Target job titles (any niche):** Founder, CEO, COO, Chief of Staff, Head of
  Operations, Operations Manager, Product Manager, Head of Product, Head of
  Customer Success, Customer Success Manager, Consulting Manager, Account
  Director, Project Manager, HR Manager, Recruiting Manager.
- **Tech-stack signal (boosts fit):** visible use of Zoom, Google Meet,
  Microsoft Teams, Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Linear, Jira, or Asana.
- **Lead scoring:** 5 = very strong fit (high meeting volume, clear
  operational/client workflow, obvious follow-up/execution pain) · 4 = good
  fit (likely frequent meetings + process coordination) · 3 = possible fit,
  pain less obvious · 2 = weak fit · 1 = not relevant.
- **Fields to capture per lead:** company name, website, company LinkedIn URL,
  industry, company size, country/city, why this company could need Laura,
  relevant pain point, best contact person, contact person's role, LinkedIn
  profile URL, work email (if available), personalization angle, lead score.

## Niche 1 — SaaS / product-led companies (10–300 employees)

**Why first:** closest match to Laura's already-built process templates
(`customer_onboarding`, `implementation_access`) and the internally validated
ICP hypothesis.

**Clay search prompt:**
> Find B2B SaaS or product-led software companies with 10–300 employees,
> headquartered or with a major office in Italy, France, Spain, Germany, UK,
> Switzerland, or the Netherlands (also include US and remote-first/
> international startups). Prioritize companies with a visible Customer
> Success or Implementation function (titles like "Customer Success Manager",
> "Onboarding Specialist", "Implementation Manager") and signals of using
> Zoom, HubSpot, Notion, Linear, or Slack. Exclude companies over 1,000
> employees, pure dev-tool/API companies with no client-facing onboarding
> motion, and companies whose main need is transcription rather than process
> follow-through.

**Pain hypothesis:** onboarding/implementation kickoff calls where a missed
step (security review, access provisioning, DPA) stalls go-live and delays
revenue recognition.

**Personalization angle:** their onboarding flow, a recent funding round, or
headcount growth (more onboarding volume = more risk of dropped steps);
position the readiness score as an early warning before a stalled onboarding
turns into churn.

## Niche 2 — Consulting & digital transformation firms (10–500 employees)

**Clay search prompt:**
> Find strategy consulting, innovation consultancy, or digital transformation
> firms with 10–500 employees, HQ or major office in Italy, France, Spain,
> Germany, UK, Switzerland, Netherlands, or the US, or explicitly
> international/multi-market consultancies. Prioritize firms running frequent
> client workshops, steering committees, or advisory calls (signals:
> "workshop", "steering committee", "client engagement" in job postings; use
> of Notion, Asana, or Jira for delivery). Exclude Big 4 / firms over 1,000
> employees unless a clearly separate innovation lab, and firms whose primary
> service is one-off delivery with minimal recurring meetings.

**Pain hypothesis:** decision-quality gaps in recurring steering/status
calls — "we'll decide next time" repeated across sessions, no clear action
owner, client asks "what did we agree" a week later.

**Personalization angle:** number of concurrent client engagements (more
parallel engagements = more meetings where decisions get lost); tie to
published case studies on transformation projects.

## Niche 3 — Marketing / creative / branding / design agencies

**Clay search prompt:**
> Find marketing, creative, branding, or design agencies with 10–300
> employees managing multiple retained clients, HQ or major office in Italy,
> France, Spain, Germany, UK, Switzerland, Netherlands, or the US. Prioritize
> agencies with an Account Management or Client Services function ("Account
> Director", "Client Services Manager", "Project Manager") and visible use of
> Slack, Asana, Notion, or HubSpot. Exclude single-founder studios with no
> account management team, and agencies over 1,000 employees.

**Pain hypothesis:** client review/status calls across many retained accounts
where action items and approvals get lost between calls, leading to missed
deadlines or scope creep with no record of client sign-off.

**Personalization angle:** client roster size or a recent new-business win
(more retained accounts = more parallel weekly status calls at risk).

---

## Niche 4 — HR, recruiting & talent agencies

**Clay search prompt:**
> Find HR consultancies, recruiting agencies, or talent/staffing firms with
> 10–300 employees running high interview/coordination call volume, HQ or
> major office in Italy, France, Spain, Germany, UK, Switzerland,
> Netherlands, or the US. Prioritize firms with titles like "Recruiting
> Manager", "Talent Acquisition Lead", "HR Manager" and signals of Notion,
> Slack, or ATS + calendar-heavy workflows. Exclude solo recruiters/
> freelancers without a team, and staffing corporations over 1,000 employees.

**Pain hypothesis:** interview debriefs and client/candidate coordination
calls where decisions (advance/reject, next step, owner) aren't consistently
captured, slowing time-to-hire and creating an inconsistent candidate
experience.

**Personalization angle:** placement volume or number of active searches
(more concurrent searches = more coordination calls where follow-through
slips).

## Suggested sequencing

Run **Niche 1** first — pull 20–30 companies, layer contact enrichment, send
a small test batch of outreach. Only expand to niches 2–4 once Niche 1's
reply data gives a read on messaging. This also lets you A/B the "readiness
score" pitch vs. the "decision follow-through" pitch across different buyer
psychologies before spending across all four niches at once.
