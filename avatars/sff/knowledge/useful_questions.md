# Useful questions and grounded answer sketches

Curated shortcuts for the questions mentors and founders ask most. Every
answer is derived from portfolio_companies.md / sff_overview.md (source:
https://sff.vc, 2026-07-06). Sector groupings use the derived sector labels.

## "What companies are in the SFF portfolio?"

The site lists 41 selected investments (33 active, 8 exited). Headline
names: Foodpanda (exited to Delivery Hero), Bring! (exited to Swiss Post),
Everphone, GuestReady, RemotePass, Polychain Capital, AirConsole (exited to
KPIT), Staking Rewards, Deskimo, PropertyScout; plus a recent AI-native
group. The full per-company list with descriptions is in
portfolio_companies.md; the site notes it is a selection, with the complete
list available from info@sff.vc.

## "Which portfolio companies are AI-related?"

Companies whose own public descriptions are explicitly AI-powered or

- Flashy, AI content operations platform for brands and marketers
- Gauss, AI-powered platform helping consumers lower credit card rates
- Inspekt AI, AI and drone-powered building facade inspection

- Stanify, AI-powered social media community management for DTC brands
- WolfPak, AI-powered leadership development and training

Adjacent but not described as AI by the site: Staking Rewards and Polychain
Capital (crypto/data), Matter (workspace software).

## "Summarize the SFF portfolio for a mentor."

SFF is a Pre-Seed/Seed, industry-agnostic fund (CHF 100k–300k tickets)
investing globally since at least 2012. The listed portfolio clusters into:
consumer apps & marketplaces (Bring!, Yhangry, Explorest, CashYou, Poinz),
food & delivery (Foodpanda, Caterspot, Orderli, A Better Florist), PropTech &
spaces (GuestReady, PropertyScout, Propera, Reno, Deskimo, StayHopper),
HR/work tech (RemotePass, Careloop, Zenadmin, WolfPak, Matter), FinTech &
identity (Gauss, Even, Dojah, Calingo), logistics (Freterium, Oware,
Stockoss), crypto (Polychain Capital, Staking Rewards, HackVC), gaming &
sports (AirConsole, Coachbetter, GotCourts), plus EdTech (Ready), aviation
(Skylink), and fund-type holdings (Pioneer Fund). Eight listed exits,
including Foodpanda → Delivery Hero and Bring! → Swiss Post. Investment
years on the site run 2012–2026, with the newest names (RemotePass, Flashy,
WolfPak, Stanify, HackVC, Pioneer Fund, Inspekt AI) skewing heavily to AI
and funds.

## "What has SFF exited?"

Eight listed exits: Foodpanda → Delivery Hero; Bring! → Swiss Post;
AirConsole → KPIT; Careloop → Klett Verlag; GotCourts → Playtomic;
Caterspot → Boom Internet; Matter → Noii; A Better Florist (acquirer not
stated on the site).

## "What does SFF invest in / would my startup fit?"

From the site: Pre-Seed and Seed, CHF 100k–300k, industry agnostic, global.
Stated criteria: early stage, clear momentum ("show us that your model
works"), technical edge ("proprietary tech is the only defensible barrier"),
laser focus, relentless drive. Pitches go to https://pitch.sff.vc.
