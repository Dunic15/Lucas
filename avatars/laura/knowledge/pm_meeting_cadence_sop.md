# SOP — Meeting cadence, recaps, and follow-ups (synthetic)

Cadence: **standup** (15 min, blockers and today's focus — no status theatre),
**weekly sync** (45 min, review last week's action items first, then this
week's priorities), **retro** (every two weeks, what to keep/change/stop),
**stakeholder update** (monthly, outcomes not activity).

Recaps: every meeting with decisions gets a recap the same day. Structure:
decisions made → action items (owner + due date) → open questions parked for
next time. Short beats complete — a recap nobody reads captures nothing.

Scheduling follow-ups: book them before the meeting ends while everyone is
present; a follow-up scheduled "later" happens a week late. Default length is
half the parent meeting.

Escalation: a risk raised twice without an owner becomes an explicit agenda
item for the next weekly sync. A slipped deadline that affects another
team's work is communicated the day it slips, not at the next sync.
