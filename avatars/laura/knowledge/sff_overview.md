# Swiss Founders Fund (SFF) — overview

All facts below come from the public SFF website (https://sff.vc), retrieved
2026-07-06. Anything not stated there is explicitly marked.

## What SFF is

Swiss Founders Fund (SFF) is an early-stage venture firm built by
entrepreneurs, based in St. Gallen, Switzerland (Schuetzengasse 7,
9000 St. Gallen). Its tagline: "Backing the next generation" — the fund
"build[s] and invest[s] in companies with bold visions and strong founding
teams globally."

In its own words: "We back and build companies that are innovative, driven by
strong founding teams, and fast at seizing opportunities. We look for
disruptive ideas, the right mix of entrepreneurial talent, and founders who
know when to reinvent — and when simply executing better wins the market."

## Investment focus (what kind of startups SFF supports)

- Stage: Pre-Seed and Seed.
- Ticket size: CHF 100k–300k (stated on the site).
- Scope: industry agnostic and global — "We back founders across sectors and
  geographies — great companies can emerge anywhere."
- The fund's stated investment criteria ("Our Type"):
  - **Early Stage** — "We invest when the vision is just taking shape."
  - **Clear Momentum** — "Show us that your model works — no matter how."
  - **Technical Edge** — "Proprietary tech is the only defensible barrier."
  - **Laser Focus** — "One big problem solved exceptionally well."
  - **Relentless Drive** — "Hard work will always beat talent."
  - **Agnostic Scope** — sectors and geographies open.

## Team background

The site says the team is "Built by Alumni of:" Y Combinator, Rocket
Internet, UBS, KPMG, HSG (University of St. Gallen), CEMS, Wharton, and RSM.
Individual team members and mentors are NOT named on the public site.

## Portfolio at a glance

The site lists a selection of 41 investments (see portfolio_companies.md for
the full per-company detail). It spans consumer apps, marketplaces, FinTech,
PropTech, HR tech, logistics, crypto, gaming, and a growing group of
AI-native companies. Publicly listed exits include Bring! (acquired by Swiss
Post), Foodpanda (Delivery Hero), AirConsole (KPIT), Careloop (Klett Verlag),
GotCourts (Playtomic), Caterspot (Boom Internet), Matter (Noii), and
A Better Florist (acquirer not stated on the site). The site notes this is
"a selection of our investments" — the complete list is available on request.

## How Laura should help mentors and founders

- Give short, concrete answers grounded in these documents; name the company
  and its one-line description rather than talking in generalities.
- For "what's in the portfolio" questions: summarize by sector or stage, then
  offer to go deeper on any single company.
- For synergy questions: point at overlapping sectors or customer bases as
  *suggestions to explore*, never as established partnerships.
- When data is missing, unverified, or marked owner-input-needed: say exactly
  that, and point to info@sff.vc for the authoritative answer.
