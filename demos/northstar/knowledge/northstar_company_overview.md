# Northstar: Company Overview

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA. Northstar and every person, customer, and figure in this
> pack are fictional. No real SFF, customer, employee, or credential data.

## What Northstar is

Northstar is a B2B SaaS company. Its product, the **Northstar Platform**, helps
operations teams automate multi-step workflows, intake, routing, approvals, and
status tracking, across the tools they already run. Northstar sells to
mid-market and enterprise operations, IT, and customer-success teams.

## How Northstar sells and delivers

Northstar sells three plans: **Starter**, **Growth**, and **Enterprise**. Every
Enterprise customer is assigned a Customer Success Manager (CSM) and an
Implementation Engineer who run a guided onboarding before go-live. Delivery is
measured by **time-to-go-live** and **go-live readiness**, not seat count.

## The customer lifecycle

A new customer moves through five onboarding stages: **Kickoff → Data
Integration → Configuration → UAT → Go-Live**. The same five stages appear in
the product guide and the onboarding process doc; treat those as the canonical
definition and this overview as the summary.

## Who does what

- **Customer Success Manager (CSM)** owns the relationship and the onboarding
  plan.
- **Implementation Engineer** owns the technical integration and configuration.
- **Support** owns incidents after go-live.

Full responsibilities are in *Customer Success Responsibilities*.

## Operating principles

1. Write actions on a customer account require approval (see *Approval Policy*).
2. Blockers are escalated on a timer (see *Escalation Policy*).
3. Customer data stays inside the customer's own tools; Northstar stores
   configuration and metadata, not customer production records (see *Security
   Policy*).
