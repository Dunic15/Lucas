# Customer Success Responsibilities

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA. Fictional roles for the Laura demo only.

## Customer Success Manager (CSM)

Owns the customer relationship and the onboarding plan. The CSM:

- runs the kickoff and gets the mutual success plan signed;
- tracks the onboarding checklist and stage status;
- records blockers and escalates them on the *Escalation Policy* timers;
- proposes **follow-up tasks** to chase missing inputs;
- owns go-live readiness.

## Implementation Engineer

Owns the technical integration and configuration (see *Implementation
Playbook*): sandbox connection, field mapping, workflow build, and UAT support.
The engineer surfaces technical blockers to the CSM rather than working around
them.

## Support

Owns incidents **after** go-live. Not involved during onboarding.

## RACI, in one line

The CSM is **accountable** for a customer reaching go-live; the Implementation
Engineer is **responsible** for the technical work; Support is **informed**
during onboarding and **responsible** afterwards.

## Escalation contacts

A P1 blocker escalates from the CSM to the CSM's manager within 1 business day
(see *Escalation Policy → Blocker severity and timers*).
