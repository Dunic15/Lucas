# Meeting Preparation Notes: Acme Robotics Onboarding Review

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA. Fictional prep notes for the Laura demo only.

## Meeting purpose

Weekly onboarding review for Acme Robotics. Goal: confirm the current blocker,
agree the remedy, and leave with one owned follow-up action.

## Attendees (fictional)

- Dana Whitfield, Northstar CSM (owner)
- Marco Ferris, Northstar Implementation Engineer
- Priya Nandakumar: Acme VP Operations (sponsor)

## What Laura should be ready to explain

1. **Where Acme is:** onboarding has reached Data Integration and is blocked.
2. **Why:** Acme has not provisioned the WMS sandbox API credentials.
3. **Impact:** with a 2026-08-15 go-live target this is P1: go-live at risk.
4. **Readiness:** go-live readiness is 1 of 5.

## The one decision to reach

Agree to create a **follow-up task** for the CSM to chase the WMS sandbox
credentials from Acme's operations team. This is a guarded, reversible action -
preview it, get approval, then execute exactly once.

## Talking points that must be grounded

- The five onboarding stages (see *Customer Onboarding Process*).
- The blocker and its severity (see *Acme Onboarding Plan* and *Escalation
  Policy*).
- Why a follow-up task is safe to create (see *Approval Policy*).

## Out of scope for this meeting

Production credentials, external data sharing, and any write beyond the single
follow-up task. Keep the meeting to the one blocker and the one action.
